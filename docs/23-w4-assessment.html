What the scores mean

Word pass means normalized ASR words exactly match the target (WER 0). Speaker pass means similarity at least 0.75. A request passes if a tested take passes both. Humming removal is deterministic (one take per case). Custom instructions allow up to 3 seed retries per run. Batches are fresh 10-case VCTK held-out sets run end-to-end with the fix in place; v034 baselines are shown for comparison, not pooled.

Why custom-01 and custom-09 fail: the fixtures, not the pipeline

Both inputs are degraded corpus variants. custom-01 (vctk-p293_035-mic2) carries added noise at 25 dB SNR; custom-09 (vctk-p351_030-mic2) carries simulated room reverb. Production-equivalent ASR (faster-whisper small, beam 3) transcribes the input audio itself as “There is a lot of heroin in the boat.” (official transcript: “…heroin about.”) and “…Bill Gates of the theatre.” (official: “…of theatre.”), word-timestamp confirmed. The pipeline preserves the input content faithfully - verified word-for-word through the v035 transplant - across 6 generation seeds per case. The exact-match gate against the official corpus transcript is unsatisfiable on these two fixtures without relaxing the gate, which was kept off the table. The remaining lever is fixture acoustics or human listening adjudication.
