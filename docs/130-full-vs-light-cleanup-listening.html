Full vs light cleanup · matched clips for listening

Supporting page to the main enhancement review. Five recordings where the pipeline’s automatic strength selection picks the gentler “light” setting, each processed both ways so the choice can be listened to. Nothing on this page is adopted, and nothing here is a quality verdict.

StatusUnreviewed - no listening verdict exists for any clip on this page. Signal-integrity checks passed on all 10 outputs (sample-exact duration, no clipping, level targets within ceiling). The measurements below are pause-level diagnostics only; they are not quality scores and no setting is adopted from them.
Why this page existsThe product direction is one automatic cleanup mode with no user-facing strength choice. Today the pipeline chooses between full and light strength from a noise estimate. These five recordings (plus the client recording on the main page) are the ones where it currently chooses light. If one global mode is the goal, full strength has to be safe on exactly these recordings. These matched pairs let you listen to that question directly.
What the measurements say (diagnostics, not listening)Across 25 measured pause windows, full and light strength differ by 1.1 dB or less in 15 windows, and the largest difference anywhere is 2.5 dB (one Tucci pause). Against the originals: on the two noisiest recordings (Tucci, 2-Minute Clips) both strengths quiet the pauses by roughly 2-7 dB; on the quote and happiness recordings both leave pauses essentially unchanged; on the Ronaldo clip light quiets pauses 2-4 dB where full mostly leaves them (one 5.6 dB exception). Measured takeaway: on these recordings the two strengths behave almost alike in pauses, so the measured evidence raises no red flag against a single full-strength mode - and it says nothing about how the voice itself sounds. That part is listening, and it is unscored.

Provenance. All 10 processed files come from one GPU run (Nov 17, 2026, 04:39-05:14 UTC) on the production enhancement code at commit ffb35cc-era settings: chunk geometry 12.0 s / 0.5 s overlap, light wet gain 0.35, full wet gain 1.0, loudness target -16 LUFS, true-peak ceiling -1.0 dBTP. The run’s runtime crashed after completion; the files were recovered intact from the VM disk and preserved to the project Drive folder (lossless WAVs, manifest, per-request attempt logs, and the window-measurement JSON). The clips on this page are 192 kbps mp3 conversions of those lossless WAVs for browser playback; the WAVs in Drive are the reference for any close listening.

Method note. Pause windows are the 5 longest stretches found in each original between -45 and -18 dBFS (50 ms frames / 25 ms hop, merged at ≥0.25 s); the same windows are then measured on all three versions. Level differences are window RMS in dBFS.
