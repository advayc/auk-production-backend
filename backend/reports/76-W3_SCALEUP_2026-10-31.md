## Findings

- **Speed**: 1.4x is not a hard rate ceiling. It passed on all three new clips tried (w3-speed-13/14/15, one on the first attempt). The failures are clip-specific: p336_034 fails at both 1.4x (w3-speed-10, speaker 0.66-0.71) and 1.35x (w3-speed-12, best 0.713).
- **Custom instructions**: systemic limitation. Speaker identity collapses below the 0.75 gate (scores 0.18-0.72) for every phrasing tried - plain "sound more confident", identity-anchored "keep the same voice", and calm documentary narration - while transcripts stay exact. 3/17 passing after repair.
- **Humming removal**: systemic limitation. Transcript breaks whether the hum overlaps speech (controlled fixtures w3-nvdel-02..09) or sits in appended trailing silence (w3-nvdel-11..17), at speaker scores 0.56-0.92. 3/17 passing after repair.
- **Voice change** (10/12) and **whisper** (10/11): healthy; failures are clip-specific transcript or borderline-speaker cases.
